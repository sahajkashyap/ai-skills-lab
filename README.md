# AI Skills for Everyone — Module 1

**What AI Is and What It Can't Do.** A plain-language, hands-on introduction
to working with AI, built for adults with no technical background.

**[Start the lab →](https://sahajkashyap.github.io/ai-skills-lab/)** — a
self-paced hour: six exercises you do with your own AI chat window and your
own real work, ending with a self-reflection assessment that scores itself.

**[View the slide deck →](https://sahajkashyap.github.io/ai-skills-lab/slides.html)** —
the same curriculum as a 21-slide deck for teaching it live. Arrow keys to
navigate; print for a PDF.

**[Read the design notes →](DESIGN-NOTES.md)** — every revision decision,
recorded the way curriculum changes should be: what changed, then why.

## What makes it different

- **You do the work, not watch it.** Every exercise uses your real email,
  your real tasks, your real expertise. The centerpiece has you deliberately
  catch an AI making something up, so you never have to take that on faith.
- **It measures learning, not scrolling.** The closing assessment checks
  whether you caught a hallucination and can make a vague request specific —
  then hands you a downloadable record of your own work, in your own words.
- **Model-agnostic on purpose.** Works with Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, or
  Gemini. It teaches the judgment, not the buttons.
